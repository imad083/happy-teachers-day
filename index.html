Dear Phoenna,

Today, on Teachers' Day, I wanted to take a moment to appreciate you in a way that goes beyond an ordinary greeting.

Teaching is mostly invisible work. The preparation no one sees, the explanation given a third time without a sigh, and the quiet decision to believe in someone before they believe in themselves. You do all of this with a patience that is rare, and it shapes people long after the lesson is over.

I may not know every challenge that comes with being a teacher, but I hope you never underestimate the difference you make. The patience you give, the knowledge you share, and the kindness you bring into your work are all worth appreciating. I hope you always find reasons to be proud of what you do, even on the days when things feel difficult.

I hope this year gives you the rest you deserve, students who surprise you, and the time to pursue what you are curious about. I hope the effort you give so freely comes back to you in ways you can feel.

Thank you for the care, the patience, and the example you set.

With sincere appreciation,
Imaduddin Ali Muhammad

Happy Teachers' Day, Phoenna.

May you always find as much happiness in teaching as the inspiration you give to others.
